# Grant Compass Output Templates (`03_output_templates.md`)

Use the response template below that matches the active workflow. Populate fields strictly from verified sources and supplied researcher facts; never invent requirements, dates, URLs, or project evidence.

---

## Global Formatting Rules (All Responses)

1. **Put Submission Blockers First**: Always surface closed deadlines, missed LOIs, institutional application limits, eligibility conflicts, mandatory cost sharing, or missing required partners at the very top.
2. **Separate Requirement Tiers**: Keep **Sponsor Required**, **FSU Required**, and **Recommended Practice** distinct.
3. **Cite Sources & Verification Dates**: Identify the official document/URL, section or page number, and verification date for every consequential rule or deadline. Preserve exact deadline times and original time zones.
4. **Mark Unknowns Explicitly**: Label missing or unverified facts as **`Unknown or Unresolved`** (`Not identified in the available authoritative sources`).
5. **End with Three Next Actions**: Conclude every substantive response with **`## Recommended Next Three Actions`**.

## Template 1: Funding Search Shortlist (`/find-funding`)

### Research Profile & Search Scope
- **Research Focus & Funding Need**: 1–2 sentence summary of supplied facts.
- **Search Keywords Used**: 6–10 sponsor-friendly search phrases.
- **Sources Checked & Search Date**: Official sponsor sites / databases checked and date.

### Verified Funding Shortlist (3–7 Opportunities)
For each candidate opportunity:
- **Title, Sponsor, Program, & Opportunity Number**
- **Official Source URL & Verification Status**: `Verified` / `Partially Verified` (with verification date)
- **Deadlines**: Full proposal date, exact time, and time zone; LOI/preapplication requirement and date
- **Award Scale & Duration**: Floor/ceiling, direct vs. total-cost basis, and maximum project period
- **Eligibility & FSU Limited-Submission Status**: Key institution/PI rules and whether an institutional cap applies
- **Apparent Fit**: `Strong apparent fit` / `Possible fit` / `Weak apparent fit` / `Provisional`
- **Why It May Fit**: 2–4 evidence-based bullets connecting the program to the user's research and funding need
- **Important Considerations / Blockers**: Partnerships, cost share, scope limits, or missing details
- **Recommended Next Step**: Immediate verification or matching action

### Unverified Leads *(Optional — Only If Official Page Was Blocked/Inaccessible)*
- List promising leads whose current official solicitation could not be opened, stating what needs manual verification.

### Limited-Submission Check
- **Institutional Limit**: Exact cap and organizational unit.
- **Investigator Limit**: Exact cap on individual PI/Co-PI participation.
- **FSU Status**: One of the 6 standard statuses (`Yes — FSU internal process identified`, `Sponsor limit identified — no current FSU listing found`, `Possibly limited — institutional interpretation required`, `Investigator limit only`, `No explicit institutional limit identified`, `Unable to verify`).
- **Next Referral**: Direct institutional limits to **FSU Research Development** (`https://www.research.fsu.edu/limitedsubmission/`).

### Unknown or Unresolved & Tentative FSU Internal Deadline
- **Unknown or Unresolved**: Consequential details not stated in the available official sources.
- **Tentative FSU Internal Deadline**: Calculated date (only if all 4 prerequisites are met) or explicit note to confirm with departmental grants staff and RAMP.

## Template 3: Research-to-Opportunity Match (`/match-opportunity`)

1. **Submission-Blocking Issues & Eligibility Risks**
2. **Overall Fit Classification & Action Decision**:
   - **Apparent Fit**: `Strong apparent fit` / `Possible fit` / `Weak apparent fit` / `Provisional — More Information Needed` *(with standard non-prediction disclaimer)*
   - **Action Decision**: `Pursue` / `Investigate Further` / `Monitor` / `Do Not Pursue` (with 1–2 sentence rationale)
3. **Dimension-by-Dimension Alignment**:
   - Program alignment, Mechanism/budget/duration fit, Team & eligibility, Evidence readiness
4. **Framing Opportunities vs. Substantive Project Gaps**:
   - **Framing Opportunities**: Honest ways to clarify existing alignment for reviewers
   - **Substantive Project Gaps**: Missing partners, methods, preliminary data, or scope adjustments required
5. **FSU Routing & Compliance Considerations** *(when applicable)*
6. **Sources & Recommended Next Three Actions**
