# Broader Impacts Coach (`/impacts`)

## When to Apply This Skill
Trigger this skill when the user types `/impacts` or asks for coaching to develop or strengthen the **Broader Impacts**, outreach, or societal benefit section of their grant proposal.

## Step-by-Step Coaching Instructions
Do **not** write a ready-to-paste Broader Impacts paragraph for the user. Coach the researcher so they can design and write an authentic, measurable plan in their own voice:

1. **Critique the Current Draft's Broader Impacts**:
   - Identify whether the user's current text relies on generic "baseline research activities" (such as *"we will mentor two graduate students, present at conferences, and put our code on GitHub"*).
   - Explain why review panels view those as standard Intellectual Merit / project execution rather than a dedicated Broader Impacts strategy.
2. **Propose 3 Tailored Activity Frameworks (Options for the PI to Customize)**:
   - Suggest 3 concrete activity directions directly connected to the user's specific science and FSU/North Florida context, asking the PI which ones fit their lab's capacity:
     - **Option 1: Curriculum & Undergraduate Research Integration** (e.g., FSU UROP cohorts, course-based modules, or cross-disciplinary training).
     - **Option 2: Practitioner, Industry, or K-12 Partnership** (e.g., workshops or tools for state/local agencies like FWC/USFS/DOH, museum exhibits, or K-12 teacher modules).
     - **Option 3: Open Science & Usable Infrastructure** (e.g., no-code practitioner interfaces, curated benchmark datasets, or public web explorers).
3. **Build a "PI Planning & Evaluation Matrix" (Markdown Table)**:
   - Provide a planning table showing the PI what details they need to decide and fill in when writing their section:
     `Activity Direction | Target Audience & Potential Partner | Concrete Deliverable | Evaluation Metric (What the PI Should Quantify)`
4. **Sentence-by-Sentence Structural Blueprint (For the Author to Draft)**:
   - Give a 4-sentence structural outline showing the author how to organize their own ~120–150 word Broader Impacts section (Sentence 1: Overarching societal/educational theme; Sentence 2: Student/workforce training + target numbers; Sentence 3: External partner/community tool + evaluation metric; Sentence 4: Open dissemination), ending with 3 specific questions for the PI to answer before writing.
