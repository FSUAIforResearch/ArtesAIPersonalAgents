# FSU Grant Compass Routing Guide

## Purpose

Use this knowledge packet to identify the appropriate Florida State University office, role, or process for a grant-related question. This packet provides routing guidance only. It does not authorize submissions, approve eligibility, set rates, make compliance determinations, or replace current FSU policy.

## Source hierarchy

Use information in this order:

1. Current sponsor solicitation and controlling amendments.
2. Current official sponsor instructions and FAQs.
3. Current FSU policy and official Office of Research guidance.
4. Current authorized information from RAMP, the Research Administration Manual, The Hub, or another approved FSU system.
5. Researcher-supplied project information.
6. General recommendations, clearly labeled as recommendations.

If sources conflict, identify the conflict and use the controlling or more current source when that can be established. Otherwise, route the question to the responsible office.

## Primary proposal routing

### U.S. public funding

Route proposals involving U.S. federal, state, or local government funding, including public funding passed through another organization, to:

- The faculty member's departmental grants analyst or research administrator.
- FSU Sponsored Research Administration, when applicable.

Official starting point:
https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/

### Private, nonprofit, foreign government, or foreign entity funding

Route questions involving direct funding from private foundations, nonprofit organizations, for-profit organizations, foreign governments, or foreign entities according to current FSU Research Foundation guidance.

Official starting point:
https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/fsu-research-foundation/

Do not infer the responsible office only from the sponsor's name. Consider the source and flow of funds and verify current FSU routing.

### Internal FSU funding

Use the current FSU internal funding source for internal competitions, seed funding, pilot programs, and Council on Research and Creativity opportunities.

Official starting point:
https://internalfunding.research.fsu.edu/

## Routing matrix

| Question or condition | Primary route | Agent behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Public federal, state, or local funding | Department grants analyst and FSU Sponsored Research Administration | Identify the office and provide the current official source. Do not claim approval or submission. |
| Private, nonprofit, for-profit, or foreign funding | FSU Research Foundation and department grants staff | Verify current routing based on the source and flow of funds. |
| Sponsor limits institutional applications | FSU Research Development | Check the sponsor limit and current FSU limited-submission source. Absence from the FSU list does not prove the opportunity is unrestricted. |
| Funding search or opportunity identification | FSU Research Development and approved discovery sources | Help identify opportunities, then verify each candidate against the official sponsor source. |
| Current F&A, fringe, tuition, institutional identifiers, or other budget facts | Current FSU Facts Sheet, authorized grants analyst, SRA, or FSURF | Retrieve the current dated source. Do not use an old stored rate or independently approve a budget. |
| Human participants or identifiable private information | Office for Human Subjects Protection / IRB | Flag possible review and refer. Do not declare exempt, approved, or not human-subjects research. |
| Vertebrate animals | Animal Care and Use Committee | Flag possible review and refer. Do not declare approved, exempt, or outside ACUC oversight. |
| Export controls, research security, foreign influence, COI, RCR, or research integrity | Research Integrity, Security, and Ethics | Flag possible review and refer. Do not make a legal or compliance determination. |
| Biosafety, hazardous materials, radiation, or laboratory safety | Current FSU environmental health, safety, or biosafety office identified by official guidance | Provide the current referral. Do not state that the work is approved or safe to begin. |
| Intellectual property, commercialization, or invention disclosure | Current FSU commercialization or technology-transfer office | Refer before disclosing unpublished patentable information in an unapproved system. |
| RAMP creation, routing, or submission workflow | Department grants staff and current RAMP guidance | Explain the general process. Do not claim actual status unless an authorized system confirms it. |
| Subaward versus contractor classification | Department grants staff and SRA/FSURF determination process | Do not classify solely from a short description when facts are incomplete. Use the official determination process. |
| Cost sharing or institutional commitment | Department, chair/dean as applicable, and SRA/FSURF | Never promise or approve cost sharing, space, personnel, equipment, or other institutional commitments. |

## Limited submissions

When the sponsor restricts the number of applications an institution may submit:

1. State the sponsor's institutional application limit.
2. Check the current FSU limited-submission source.
3. Report the result as one of:
   - Yes - FSU internal process identified.
   - No listing identified - sponsor limit still requires verification.
   - Possibly limited - sponsor language is unclear.
   - Unable to verify.
4. If the sponsor imposes a limit and no FSU listing is found, refer the researcher to FSU Research Development immediately.

Official source:
https://www.research.fsu.edu/limitedsubmission/

## Proposal submission timing

For proposals subject to FSU's proposal-submission policy, distinguish the sponsor deadline from the FSU internal deadline. Use the current controlling FSU policy and university closure calendar. Do not subtract three calendar days.

When calculating a tentative internal deadline:

1. Confirm the sponsor date, exact time, and time zone.
2. Confirm whether FSU is the lead applicant or a subrecipient.
3. Account for weekends and official university closures.
4. Apply the current FSU working-day policy.
5. Label the date tentative until confirmed through RAMP, the departmental grants analyst, SRA, or FSURF.

## Compliance language

Use language such as:

> This project appears to involve [human participants / vertebrate animals / international activity / controlled information]. Current FSU institutional requirements may apply. Consult [responsible office] before beginning covered work. Grant Compass is flagging a possible review need, not making a compliance determination.

Do not say:

- The project is exempt.
- No review is required.
- The project is approved.
- The activity is not export controlled.
- The researcher is eligible to begin work.

## Missing or uncertain routing information

If the responsible office cannot be established from authoritative sources, say:

> The appropriate FSU routing office was not identified in the available authoritative sources. Consult the departmental grants analyst or FSU Office of Research for current routing.

## Maintenance rule

Review this packet at least quarterly and whenever FSU changes office responsibilities, proposal-submission policy, limited-submission procedures, RAMP routing, or compliance-office structure. Do not place current rates, thresholds, passwords, or confidential system information in this file.
