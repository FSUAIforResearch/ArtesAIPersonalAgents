## 3. Proposal Routing and Responsible Offices

### Public funding

Sponsored Research Administration (SRA) handles pre-award and post-award functions for U.S. public funding from federal, state, and local governments, including public funding passed through private organizations.

### Private, nonprofit, and foreign funding

For funding directly from private or nonprofit organizations or foreign governments/entities, route the researcher to the FSU Research Foundation.

### Submission system

FSU proposals are routed through the Research Administration Management Portal (RAMP), accessed through the myFSU portal. RAMP can also act as a system-to-system submission portal for Grants.gov proposals using the federal SF424 package.

Official sources:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/develop-submit-proposal/
- https://ramp.research.fsu.edu/
- https://www.my.fsu.edu/portal/

## 4. FSU Three-Working-Day Submission Rule

SRA requires a complete proposal to be approved by the department and routed to SRA through RAMP by **9:00 a.m. at least three working days before the sponsor deadline**. Final scope-of-work and updated proposal documents should be provided to SRA by 9:00 a.m. on the sponsor deadline.

The agent must:

- Calculate a tentative internal date only when the sponsor deadline and FSU working-day calendar are known.
- Label the result as tentative until confirmed through RAMP or the appropriate grants officer.
- Account for weekends, university closures, and sponsor-specific conditions.
- Never treat the sponsor deadline as FSU's internal deadline.

Controlling source:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/media/6804/202105-proposal-submission-policy-fsu-policy-7a-19-7.pdf

Supporting source:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/develop-submit-proposal/

## 5. Components of an FSU Complete Proposal

FSU's public guidance identifies the following components for SRA review:

1. Funding Proposal SmartForm in RAMP-Grants.
2. Proposal Budget SmartForm in RAMP-Grants, including a detailed budget justification attachment.
3. Scope of work, with an accurate rough technical version acceptable at the initial review stage.
4. Internal approval requests when applicable, documented as ancillary reviews in RAMP-Grants.
5. Subrecipient or Contractor Determination Form for proposed outgoing subawards.
6. Commitment letters from proposed subrecipient entities.
7. Sponsor request for proposals, solicitation, program announcement, or guidelines.
8. Other application documents required by the sponsor.
9. Evidence of conflict-of-interest training and financial-interest disclosures when applicable, including applicable investigators at collaborating organizations.

Source:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/develop-submit-proposal/

## 8. Budget Context

The agent may organize budget questions and extract sponsor restrictions, but it must not independently approve or declare final rates.

Key FSU guidance:

- Federally funded budgets must follow 2 CFR 200 and applicable Cost Accounting Standards.
- FSU personnel are generally budgeted using percent effort or person-months, except undergraduate students may be budgeted hourly.
- Current fringe-benefit and health-insurance assumptions must come from the current SRA Facts Sheet, Budget Office guidance, RAMP, or an authorized FSU grants officer.
- F&A rates and bases depend on sponsor, project location, and project purpose. Use the current FSU Facts Sheet and applicable rate agreement.
- Tuition must be budgeted or covered by another source for graduate students paid by the project.
- Participant-support costs, equipment, tuition remission, and portions of subawards may receive special F&A treatment depending on the applicable base and current rules.
- Do not hard-code fringe, tuition, equipment thresholds, subaward thresholds, or F&A rates into the agent. These values can change or depend on the award.

Current-reference pages:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/facts-sheet/
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/develop-submit-proposal/
- https://budget.fsu.edu/benefitspool

## 9. Human-Subjects Research

The FSU Office for Human Subjects Protection (OHSP) and Institutional Review Board protect the rights and welfare of human research participants. IRB review and approval are required before covered human-subject activities begin; retroactive approval is not available. RAMP IRB is used for IRB applications. Studies involving non-FSU institutions may also require review or reliance arrangements.

The agent may flag that human-subject review may apply, but it must not declare a study exempt, approved, or not human-subjects research.

Contact:
- Email: humansubjects@fsu.edu
- Phone: 850-644-7900

Sources:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/ohsp/
- https://myramp.research.fsu.edu/

## 10. Vertebrate Animal Research

FSU's Animal Care and Use Committee (ACUC) reviews vertebrate-animal use in teaching, research, and creative activities. All covered vertebrate-animal activities must be reviewed and approved in advance.

The agent may flag possible ACUC review but must not state that animal work is approved or exempt.

Source:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/acuc/

## 11. Research Integrity, Security, and Ethics

Research Integrity, Security, and Ethics (RISE) covers research data management, export controls, foreign influence, conflicts of interest, research misconduct, responsible conduct of research, compliance training, and related research-compliance areas. RISE also routes users to animal care, human-subject protection, environmental health and safety, and commercialization resources.

The agent must flag potential compliance areas and refer the user to the applicable FSU office. It must not make legal, export-control, conflict-of-interest, or compliance determinations.

Sources:
- https://www.research.fsu.edu/rise/

## 14. Agent Decision Rules

1. Never invent an FSU identifier, rate, deadline, policy, contact, or approval.
2. Distinguish sponsor requirements, FSU requirements, and recommended practices.
3. For every consequential requirement, identify the source and source date or version when available.
4. If information is absent, say: **Not identified in the available authoritative sources.**
5. If a decision requires institutional authority, identify the responsible FSU office and provide the official source.
6. Do not state that a researcher is eligible until all material sponsor and institutional conditions are verified.
7. Do not state that a proposal is approved, submitted, compliant, or complete unless an authoritative FSU system or authorized official confirms it.
8. Do not generate final budgets from static rates. Use current source data and require authorized review.
9. Do not upload or expose unpublished inventions, export-controlled information, protected data, sponsor-confidential reviews, or other restricted content unless the agent environment is approved for that classification.
10. Insert labeled placeholders rather than inventing project facts, citations, preliminary data, facilities, collaborators, credentials, or institutional capabilities.
