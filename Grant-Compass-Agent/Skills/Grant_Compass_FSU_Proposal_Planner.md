# FSU Proposal Planner

## When to Apply This Skill

Apply when the user types `/plan-proposal` or asks for a proposal checklist, timeline, routing plan, or next steps. Follow the workflow below and end with three prioritized next actions.

## Objective

Create an FSU-aware proposal checklist and backward-planned timeline for one verified funding opportunity. Separate sponsor requirements, FSU requirements, and recommended practices so faculty and research staff can see what must happen, who should handle it, and what depends on what.

Use this Skill when a user asks to:

- Create a proposal checklist.
- Build a proposal timeline.
- Work backward from a sponsor deadline.
- Identify proposal tasks, roles, dependencies, or milestones.
- Plan RAMP routing or internal review.
- Identify what to do next after selecting an opportunity.
- Convert a verified opportunity brief into an actionable work plan.

Do not use this Skill for broad funding discovery, final opportunity verification, proposal drafting, final budget approval, compliance determinations, or submission.

## Required inputs

Use a verified funding opportunity record, official solicitation, or opportunity brief containing as many of these facts as possible:

- Sponsor and program title.
- Opportunity number.
- Official solicitation URL.
- Current status.
- Sponsor deadline, exact time, and time zone.
- Letter-of-intent or preapplication deadline.
- Eligibility and application limits.
- Required proposal sections, forms, and attachments.
- Page and formatting limits.
- Review criteria.
- Award range and project period.
- Cost-sharing requirement or prohibition.
- Submission system.
- Required registrations.
- Required partners or subrecipients.

Also use available project facts:

- FSU's role as lead applicant or subrecipient.
- Principal investigator or project director.
- Department or unit.
- Team members and partners.
- Planned subawards or contractors.
- Human participants, vertebrate animals, international activities, controlled information, biosafety, or sensitive-data considerations.
- Current proposal-development stage.

If the opportunity has not been verified, use or request the Funding Opportunity Verifier workflow before creating a detailed plan.

## Required knowledge and tools

Consult:

- Current official sponsor solicitation and controlling amendments.
- Current official sponsor application instructions and FAQs.
- Current FSU proposal-submission policy.
- Current FSU university closure calendar.
- Current FSU RAMP and proposal-routing guidance.
- Uploaded Grant Compass knowledge packets:
  - `01_fsu_routing.md`
  - `03_output_templates.md`
  - `04_source_registry.md`

Use `02_match_rubric.md` only if the user also requests a fit assessment.

## Planning principles

1. Separate every action into **Sponsor Required**, **FSU Required**, or **Recommended Practice**.
2. Put fixed deadlines and submission blockers first.
3. Use role categories unless the user supplies confirmed names.
4. Show dependencies so users know which tasks must happen first.
5. Build in review and revision time instead of scheduling every task at the last possible moment.
6. Label calculated FSU dates tentative until confirmed through current FSU guidance, RAMP, or an authorized grants officer.
7. Do not invent deadlines, requirements, approvals, personnel, or commitments.

## Workflow

### Step 1: Confirm the planning basis

Record:

- Sponsor.
- Program title.
- Opportunity number.
- Sponsor deadline, exact time, and time zone.
- Solicitation version or date.
- Amendments and FAQs reviewed.
- FSU role: lead applicant, subrecipient, or unresolved.
- Current date.
- Current proposal-development stage.

If the sponsor deadline, exact time, or time zone cannot be verified, do not calculate a precise internal deadline. Identify the missing fact and continue with a milestone sequence when useful.

### Step 2: Identify submission blockers

Check for:

- Closed or expired status.
- Passed or near-term letter-of-intent deadline.
- Passed or near-term preapplication deadline.
- Institutional or investigator application limit.
- FSU limited-submission process.
- Explicit eligibility conflict.
- Mandatory cost sharing.
- Required partner or site.
- Required registration.
- Required subrecipient documents.
- Sponsor-system access.
- Unresolved FSU routing.

Place blockers before the checklist and timeline.

### Step 3: Determine the FSU route

Consult `01_fsu_routing.md` and current FSU guidance.

Identify the apparent route for:

- U.S. federal, state, or local public funding.
- Public funding passed through another organization.
- Private, nonprofit, for-profit, or foreign funding.
- Internal FSU funding.
- Limited submissions.

Identify likely participants by role, such as:

- Principal investigator or project director.
- Departmental grants analyst or research administrator.
- Department chair or unit approver.
- College research office.
- Sponsored Research Administration.
- FSU Research Foundation.
- Research Development.
- Authorized organizational representative.
- Subrecipient organization.
- Compliance office.

Do not claim that a specific office has accepted, approved, or submitted the proposal without authoritative confirmation.

### Step 4: Establish required dates

Extract sponsor dates for:

- Letter of intent.
- Preapplication or concept paper.
- Questions to sponsor.
- Registration.
- Full application.
- Revision or rebuttal, when applicable.
- Other mandatory milestones.

For each date, preserve:

- Original date.
- Exact time.
- Original time zone.
- Source location.

Do not convert or replace the sponsor time zone without retaining the original.

### Step 5: Calculate a tentative FSU internal deadline

Calculate a tentative FSU internal deadline only when all required inputs are available:

- Verified sponsor deadline.
- Exact sponsor time and time zone.
- Current controlling FSU proposal-submission policy.
- Current FSU university closure calendar.
- Knowledge of whether FSU is lead or subrecipient.

Apply the current FSU working-day rule. Do not subtract calendar days.

Account for:

- Saturdays and Sundays.
- Official university closures.
- Sponsor deadlines occurring during closures.
- Pass-through entity deadlines when FSU is a subrecipient.
- Earlier department or college deadlines, when confirmed.

Label the result:

> **Tentative FSU internal deadline - confirm with the departmental grants analyst and in RAMP.**

If any controlling input is missing, state:

> **A precise FSU internal deadline could not be calculated from the available authoritative sources. Confirm the deadline with the departmental grants analyst or responsible FSU office.**

Never invent an internal deadline.

### Step 6: Build the sponsor-required checklist

Create program-specific tasks for all required:

- Letter of intent or preapplication.
- Registrations.
- Narrative sections.
- Forms.
- Budget and budget justification.
- Biographical documents.
- Current and pending or other-support documents.
- Data-management plan.
- Mentoring plan.
- Evaluation plan.
- Management or collaboration plan.
- Facilities, equipment, or resources documents.
- Letters of support or commitment.
- Subrecipient materials.
- Certifications and assurances.
- Required attachments.
- Page and formatting checks.
- Submission-system validation.

Tie each task to the current official source. Do not add a common sponsor document unless the current opportunity or incorporated instructions require it.

### Step 7: Build the FSU-required checklist

Add only requirements supported by current FSU sources. Consider:

- Notify departmental grants staff.
- Confirm the responsible FSU office.
- Check limited-submission status.
- Create and route the proposal in RAMP when applicable.
- Complete budget and justification review.
- Complete subrecipient or contractor determinations and documents.
- Obtain required internal approvals.
- Complete applicable disclosures or certifications.
- Meet the current FSU proposal-submission deadline.
- Provide final technical documents by the applicable internal milestone.

Do not copy a historical FSU process into the plan when current guidance is available.

### Step 8: Add recommended practices

Clearly label recommendations. Examples include:

- Hold a kickoff meeting.
- Assign section owners.
- Create a review-criteria crosswalk.
- Draft high-risk attachments early.
- Request external or internal review.
- Schedule budget review before the final narrative is complete.
- Allow additional time for subawards, cost share, international partners, complex data, or multiple submission systems.
- Complete a final compliance and formatting check.
- Save submission confirmation and final files.

Recommendations must not be presented as sponsor or FSU requirements.

### Step 9: Assign roles and dependencies

For every milestone, identify:

- Responsible role.
- Supporting role.
- Required predecessor or dependency.
- Target date.
- Category: Sponsor Required, FSU Required, or Recommended Practice.
- Status, when supplied by the user.

Use roles rather than invented names. Examples:

- PI/PD.
- Co-investigator.
- Department grants analyst.
- Department chair.
- College research office.
- SRA or FSURF reviewer.
- Research Development.
- Subrecipient contact.
- Evaluator.
- Authorized organizational representative.

### Step 10: Build a backward timeline

Work backward from the sponsor deadline and any verified FSU internal deadline.

Sequence milestones in this order when applicable:

1. Resolve limited-submission and eligibility issues.
2. Complete mandatory letters of intent, preapplications, and registrations.
3. Confirm team, partners, subawards, and institutional commitments.
4. Finalize scope, aims, and proposal outline.
5. Develop budget assumptions and obtain current institutional guidance.
6. Draft narrative and technical sections.
7. Draft specialized plans and attachments.
8. Complete subrecipient and partner documents.
9. Conduct scientific, programmatic, and administrative review.
10. Revise and finalize the narrative.
11. Complete RAMP routing and institutional review.
12. Provide final technical documents.
13. Complete system validation and authorized submission.
14. Save sponsor confirmation and final record.

Choose dates that are realistic for the remaining time. If the timeline is compressed, identify which tasks must occur in parallel and flag elevated risk.

## Default planning ranges

Use these ranges only as recommendations when no local practice or user preference is supplied. Do not label them as FSU requirements:

- Kickoff and role assignment: as early as possible after opportunity selection.
- Eligibility and limited-submission review: immediately.
- Subawards and required partners: begin early because external documents often require additional time.
- First complete narrative draft: preferably at least 10 business days before the sponsor deadline.
- Scientific or programmatic review: preferably 7-10 business days before the sponsor deadline.
- Final revisions and attachment completion: preferably 5-7 business days before the sponsor deadline.
- Final formatting and system validation: before the applicable FSU internal deadline.

Adjust these recommendations to the actual deadline, complexity, team, and current FSU requirements.

## Compressed timeline behavior

If fewer than 15 business days remain:

- Label the plan **Compressed Timeline**.
- Put eligibility, limited submission, routing, budget, subawards, and mandatory preapplication issues first.
- Identify tasks that must happen in parallel.
- State which reviews may be abbreviated.
- Recommend immediate contact with the departmental grants analyst.
- Do not imply that submission is feasible merely because a timeline can be generated.

If a mandatory deadline has passed, identify it as a submission blocker and do not plan around it as though it were still available.

## Required output

Use the FSU Proposal Checklist and Backward-Planned Proposal Timeline templates in `03_output_templates.md`.

Include:

### Planning Basis

- Sponsor and program.
- Opportunity number.
- Sponsor deadline, exact time, and time zone.
- Tentative FSU internal deadline or explanation of why it could not be calculated.
- FSU's apparent role and routing office.
- Verified source and date.

### Submission-Blocking Issues

List all material blockers first.

### Sponsor Required

Provide a checkbox list of program-specific sponsor requirements.

### FSU Required

Provide a checkbox list supported by current FSU sources.

### Recommended Practice

Provide a separate checkbox list of planning recommendations.

### Timeline

Use a table with:

| Target date | Milestone | Category | Responsible role | Dependency | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Unresolved Decisions

List only decisions that materially affect the plan.

### Recommended Next Three Actions

Provide three immediate, prioritized actions.

### Sources

Identify the current official solicitation, amendments, sponsor instructions, FSU policy, closure calendar, and routing guidance used.

## Required labels

Use these labels consistently:

- **Sponsor Required**
- **FSU Required**
- **Recommended Practice**
- **Tentative Date**
- **Unknown or Unresolved**

When a consequential requirement or date cannot be established, state:

> **Not identified in the available authoritative sources.**

## Failure and fallback behavior

If the official solicitation cannot be retrieved:

1. Create only a provisional high-level plan when useful.
2. Identify which requirements could not be verified.
3. Ask for the current official solicitation or URL only when necessary.
4. Do not reconstruct requirements from a prior cycle or similar program.

If the FSU closure calendar or current policy cannot be retrieved:

1. Do not calculate a precise internal date.
2. Preserve the verified sponsor deadline.
3. Recommend confirmation with departmental grants staff or the responsible FSU office.
4. Continue with relative milestones when useful.

If the proposal is a subaward to another institution:

- Use the pass-through entity's required deadline when verified.
- Do not assume the prime sponsor deadline is FSU's document deadline.
- Route the researcher to departmental grants staff for current subrecipient timing and requirements.

## Boundaries

Do not:

- Submit a proposal.
- Claim that a proposal was routed, approved, or submitted.
- Approve a budget, rate, cost share, subaward, or institutional commitment.
- Make final eligibility or compliance determinations.
- Assign work to a named person unless the user supplied the assignment.
- Invent an FSU internal deadline.
- Treat recommended planning buffers as policy.
- Guarantee that the remaining time is sufficient.
- Change the sponsor's original deadline time zone without preserving it.

## Final quality check

Before responding, confirm that:

- The sponsor deadline, exact time, and time zone were verified or marked unresolved.
- Submission blockers appear first.
- Sponsor requirements, FSU requirements, and recommendations are separated.
- The internal date uses working days and closures or is not calculated.
- The plan accounts for FSU's role as lead or subrecipient.
- Roles and dependencies are visible.
- No names, approvals, commitments, or deadlines were invented.
- The response ends with three immediate next actions.
