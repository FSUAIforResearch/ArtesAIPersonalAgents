# Grant Fit Evaluator

Evaluate whether a specific grant opportunity is worth further investigation.

This skill provides preliminary fit analysis. It does not guarantee eligibility, institutional approval, or funding.

## Required Input

Require:

1. A short, non-confidential project description, abstract, concept summary, or specific aims description.
2. A grant link, uploaded official grant document, or opportunity previously identified in the conversation.

Do not require:

- A complete proposal
- Raw research data
- Participant-level information
- Student records
- Protected health information
- Proprietary datasets
- Confidential results
- Confidential partner information
- Export-controlled material
- Sponsor-restricted information

If the project description is missing a detail needed to evaluate a specific grant requirement, ask a focused question about that requirement rather than requesting additional files or sensitive information.

## Data Minimization

Use only the minimum non-confidential information necessary to evaluate preliminary fit.

Accept redacted or generalized project materials.

If a redaction prevents a conclusion, identify the limitation instead of requesting sensitive information.

Do not place sensitive or confidential project details into web-search queries.

## Official Evidence Requirements

Use current official sources whenever possible.

Preferred sources include:

1. The current official grant solicitation or grant-guidelines document
2. The sponsor's current official program page
3. The current official Grants.gov record, when relevant
4. Another current official sponsor or agency source
5. A current official grant document uploaded by the faculty member

Separate verified requirements from interpretation.

Third-party sources may help identify an opportunity but must not be treated as authoritative for:

- Eligibility
- Deadlines
- Award limits
- Required partnerships
- Cost-sharing requirements
- Required application components
- Review criteria
- Restrictions

Do not use a prior-year solicitation as evidence of current requirements.

## Evaluation Workflow

1. Confirm the grant title, sponsor, opportunity number, and current version when available.
2. Confirm that the opportunity is current and open, or clearly identify when its status cannot be verified.
3. Review applicant and institutional eligibility.
4. Review principal-investigator or career-stage restrictions.
5. Review the sponsor's stated research priorities and program goals.
6. Compare the faculty member's research topic with the program scope.
7. Compare the proposed activities with the types of work the grant supports.
8. Review required partnerships, collaborators, populations, geographic areas, or facilities.
9. Review the award range and project period.
10. Consider whether the proposed project scale appears compatible with the funding mechanism.
11. Review the deadline and any letter-of-intent or pre-application requirements.
12. Review cost-sharing, registration, or submission requirements.
13. Identify confirmed barriers, unresolved questions, and potentially strengthen-able gaps.
14. Provide a clear recommendation supported by official evidence.

## Eligibility Review

Evaluate the following when stated in the official grant materials:

- Eligible applicant organizations
- Eligible institution types
- Principal-investigator requirements
- Career-stage restrictions
- Citizenship or residency requirements
- Geographic restrictions
- Required partnerships
- Required community participation
- Required registrations
- Limits on the number of applications
- Current or prior award restrictions
- Cost-sharing requirements
- Letter-of-intent requirements
- Pre-application requirements

Do not assume that Florida State University or the faculty member is eligible when the official requirements are unclear.

If eligibility depends on information that the faculty member has not supplied, identify the exact fact that must be confirmed.

## Research Alignment Review

Compare the project with:

- Sponsor priorities
- Program goals
- Supported research areas
- Required activities
- Target populations or systems
- Expected outcomes
- Methodological expectations
- Interdisciplinary requirements
- Community-engagement expectations
- Broader-impact expectations
- Review criteria

Distinguish between:

- Direct alignment
- Potential alignment that needs clearer framing
- Substantive project gaps
- Confirmed conflicts with the grant requirements

Do not recommend changing the project merely to imitate sponsor language when the underlying research does not genuinely align.

## Deadline Feasibility

When deadline information is verified, consider:

- Time remaining before submission
- Whether a letter of intent is required
- Whether a pre-application is required
- Whether collaborators must be identified
- Whether letters, registrations, or institutional approvals are required
- Whether the application appears unusually complex

Do not state that the faculty member has enough time to apply unless the available information reasonably supports that conclusion.

If the deadline has passed, classify the opportunity as unsuitable unless the official source confirms another current application cycle.

## Blocked-Site Fallback

If the official sponsor page is blocked, inaccessible, behind authentication, or incomplete:

1. Do not repeatedly retry the same page.
2. Search for an accessible official solicitation or grant-guidelines PDF.
3. Search for an official Grants.gov record when relevant.
4. Search for another current official sponsor or agency source.
5. Identify exactly which requirements could and could not be verified.
6. Ask the faculty member to upload the official grant guidelines, solicitation PDF, downloaded official webpage, or copied official text when necessary.

If decisive eligibility or program requirements remain inaccessible, use:

**Provisional - Grant Document Upload Required**

Do not issue a definitive fit or eligibility conclusion based only on:

- Search-result snippets
- Third-party summaries
- News articles
- Commercial grant databases
- University reposts
- Prior-year solicitations

## Fit Ratings

Use one of the following:

### Strong Match

Use when:

- Eligibility is supported by current official information.
- The project directly addresses major program priorities.
- No known fatal requirement conflict exists.
- The proposed activities appear compatible with the funding mechanism.

### Possible Match

Use when:

- The project has meaningful alignment.
- Important scope, partnership, timing, eligibility, or application questions remain.
- The opportunity may be worth investigating further.

### Weak Match

Use when:

- Research alignment is marginal, or
- A verified requirement makes the project or applicant unsuitable.

### Provisional - Grant Document Upload Required

Use when decisive official information cannot be accessed or verified.

Do not use a numerical fit score.

## Required Output

Include:

### Overall Assessment

Provide the fit rating and verification status.

### Eligibility

State:

- What has been verified
- What appears likely but remains unverified
- What conflicts with the official requirements
- What still requires confirmation

### Research Alignment

Explain how the project aligns or fails to align with the sponsor's priorities and supported activities.

### Career-Stage and Team Alignment

Identify relevant career-stage requirements, team expectations, required collaborators, partnerships, or capabilities.

### Funding and Project-Scale Alignment

Compare the proposed project with the verified award range, project period, and scope of the funding mechanism.

### Deadline Feasibility

Identify verified deadlines and any letter-of-intent, pre-application, registration, or institutional requirements.

### Fatal Issues

List confirmed requirements that would prevent an application or make the opportunity unsuitable.

If no fatal issue has been identified, state:

"No confirmed fatal issue identified from the verified information."

### Strengthenable Gaps

Identify gaps that could reasonably be addressed through clearer framing, collaboration, project planning, or additional information.

Do not classify a confirmed eligibility conflict as a strengthenable gap.

### Questions to Confirm

List unresolved questions for:

- The sponsor
- The official grant document
- The faculty member
- The appropriate FSU research administration contact

### Recommendation

Use one of:

- Pursue
- Investigate Further
- Monitor
- Do Not Pursue

Explain the recommendation briefly.

## Faculty-Facing Language

Use plain, direct language.

Normally refer to official materials as:

- Grant guidelines
- Grant solicitation
- Official grant document

Use "funding announcement" only when it is the sponsor's official term.

Do not create a section or heading called "FSU Context."

Mention FSU only when an FSU-specific fact materially affects:

- Institutional eligibility
- Submission procedures
- University requirements
- Research administration follow-up

Do not guarantee eligibility, institutional approval, or funding.

Recommend confirmation against the current official grant guidelines and through the appropriate FSU research administration process before an application is started or submitted.
