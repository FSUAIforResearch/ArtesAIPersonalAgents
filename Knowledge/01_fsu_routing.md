# FSU Routing and Institutional Boundaries

## Why Grant Compass Uses This Knowledge

Funding analysis must lead to the right institutional next step. Use this reference to identify apparent routing and possible review needs while reserving institutional decisions for authorized FSU people. It supports discovery, verification, matching, limited-submission checking, planning, and outlining.

## Source Order

Use current official sponsor solicitations and controlling amendments for sponsor requirements. Use current official FSU policies and authorized institutional records for FSU requirements. Researcher-supplied facts describe the project; general advice remains a recommendation. If controlling sources conflict and the conflict cannot be resolved, identify it and refer the question.

## Routing Reference

| Question or project condition | Apparent route from the existing Grant Compass routing packet | How to use it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| U.S. federal, state, or local public funding, including public pass-through funding | Departmental grants staff and Sponsored Research Administration, as applicable. | Verify current routing and the source and flow of funds. |
| Direct private, nonprofit, for-profit, or foreign funding | Departmental grants staff and current FSU Research Foundation guidance. | Do not infer the route from the sponsor's name alone. |
| Institutional application or nomination limit | Research Development and the current FSU limited-submission process. | Identify the exact limit and the unit it applies to. |
| Budget, F&A, fringe, tuition, cost sharing, or institutional identifiers | Current FSU Facts Sheet and responsible grants staff or office. | Obtain current applicable values; do not approve budgets or commitments. |
| Human participants or identifiable private information | Office for Human Subjects Protection / current IRB process. | Flag possible review; do not decide exemption or approval. |
| Vertebrate animals | Animal Care and Use Committee. | Refer oversight questions; do not authorize covered work. |
| Research integrity, security, export controls, or conflicts of interest | Research Integrity, Security, and Ethics, according to current guidance. | Flag possible review without a legal or compliance determination. |
| RAMP routing, subrecipient documentation, or contractor classification | Departmental grants staff and the responsible SRA/FSURF process. | Confirm applicable procedures and system status through authorized sources. |
| Biosafety, laboratory safety, or intellectual property | Responsible office identified by current official FSU guidance. | Do not invent a contact or declare the activity cleared. |

These are routing references adapted from the existing knowledge packet, not newly verified office assignments. Consult the active source registry before live retrieval.

## Limited-Submission Knowledge

Read institutional limits separately from investigator limits. Identify whether a limit applies to the institution, campus, college, department, nomination, LOI, preapplication, or PI role.

If a sponsor limits institutional submissions, check the current FSU process using permitted sources. A missing public listing does not establish unrestricted submission. If the process is unresolved, refer to Research Development and do not imply that the researcher is selected or authorized to apply.

## Internal-Date Prerequisites

Before calculating a tentative FSU internal deadline, establish:

1. The verified sponsor deadline, exact time, and original time zone.
2. Whether FSU is lead applicant or subrecipient.
3. The current controlling FSU working-day policy and applicable university closures.
4. Any confirmed earlier departmental, college, or pass-through document deadline.

Apply working days and closures as the current policy requires. Do not substitute calendar-day subtraction. When FSU is a subrecipient, use the verified pass-through deadline that applies to FSU's materials. Label a calculated date tentative until confirmed through the responsible grants staff or authorized institutional record.

When inputs are missing, say: **A precise FSU internal deadline could not be calculated from the available authoritative sources.** Continue with relative milestones.

## Response Boundaries

Separate **Sponsor Required**, **FSU Required**, and **Recommended Practice**. Present an item as FSU Required only when supported by current authoritative FSU evidence. Do not claim that a proposal is routed, approved, submitted, or accepted without authoritative confirmation. Do not approve budgets, cost sharing, subawards, compliance, or institutional commitments.

Request only nonconfidential facts needed for the task. Do not ask for participant records, credentials, controlled information, proprietary details, or unpublished patentable material.
