# Sources, Evidence, and Verification

## Why Grant Compass Uses This Knowledge

Funding status and institutional requirements change. This reference helps all six skills distinguish discovery leads, controlling requirements, researcher facts, and recommendations. It prevents static knowledge from being treated as evidence of a current deadline or approval.

## Active Source Configuration

The detailed **Grant Compass Approved Source Registry**, maintained at `Grant-Compass-Agent/Knowledge files/04_source_registry.md` in this repository, holds source-specific URLs, statuses, page limits, testing notes, and ownership. This compact file does not replicate that changing approval record.

For live work, use the detailed registry as the active uploaded file in place of this compact guide. If the detailed registry is unavailable, do not assume its source permissions or page limits. Continue with supplied documents, label current status unresolved, and request the active configuration when live retrieval is needed.

## Source Roles in the Existing Design

| Source category | Why the agent uses it | Evidence boundary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Official sponsor solicitation and controlling amendments | Establish program-specific eligibility, deadlines, application limits, funding, components, and review criteria. | Confirm the correct competition and current controlling version. |
| Incorporated sponsor application instructions and program FAQs | Resolve requirements distributed across multiple sponsor documents. | Identify which instructions are incorporated and any controlling exceptions. |
| Official FSU research guidance, policies, and applicable calendar | Establish institutional routing, current policy, and internal-date inputs. | A local summary or historical PDF cannot establish current applicability alone. |
| FSU limited-submission source and Research Development guidance | Identify internal selection procedures where sponsor limits apply. | Absence from a listing does not remove the sponsor's limit. |
| Current FSU Facts Sheet and authorized grants staff | Resolve institutional facts and applicable budget assumptions. | Do not cache changing rates as timeless knowledge or approve budget treatment. |
| Approved discovery APIs and databases | Find candidate opportunities efficiently. | Records are leads; verify consequential requirements against official sponsor evidence. |
| Researcher-provided documents and project facts | Supply the specific solicitation, concept, team, stage, and proposed scope. | An uploaded document is not automatically current, and proposed commitments are not confirmed commitments. |

The detailed registry includes FSU Office of Research, SRA, Research Development, limited submissions, internal funding, RAMP, and relevant compliance sources; federal discovery and sponsor sources include Grants.gov, NIH, NSF, and other listed agencies. Their inclusion here describes the existing design and does not grant retrieval approval.

## Status Rules

| Status in the active detailed registry | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Approved | Tested and reliable for its documented purpose and scope. |
| Conditional | Use only within the documented limitation or required human-verification condition. |
| Testing | Not approved for routine faculty-facing live retrieval; use only in the owner's acceptance-testing workflow. |
| Removed | Do not use. |

Respect source-specific retrieval limits. Do not silently promote a Testing source. A stored “last tested” date is not proof that a particular grant or policy was verified in the current session.

## Evidence Record

For each consequential claim, retain the source title, URL if one exists, version or publication date when available, page or section, actual retrieval/verification date, and any limitation. Preserve deadline time and time zone and distinguish current information from archives.

Search snippets, newsletters, third-party listings, API summaries, old successful proposals, and prior-cycle notices cannot override current official requirements. If an amendment controls, record that fact. If control or currency is unresolved, identify the conflicting statements and refer the question rather than inventing a resolution.

## Retrieval Failure and Exercise Material

When a source cannot be opened, identify it and mark affected facts **Unknown or Unresolved**. Continue supported analysis, request the current official document when necessary, and do not reconstruct missing rules from a similar program. Do not describe a search as exhaustive.

For a supplied fictional training packet, identify the packet and its sections as exercise evidence. Never fabricate an official URL, current open status, live verification date, institutional selection, or approval. Do not place fictional opportunities in a real verified shortlist.

## Maintenance

Maintain live approvals and acceptance-test outcomes in the detailed registry. Its acceptance checks cover opening the intended page, retrieving relevant text and linked PDFs, distinguishing current and archived content, reporting source URLs and versions, applying retrieval limits, and handling failure without invention. Keep credentials and confidential configuration out of both registry versions.
