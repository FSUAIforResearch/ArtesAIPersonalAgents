# Research-to-Opportunity Match Rubric

## Why Grant Compass Uses This Knowledge

Shared keywords do not establish that a funding mechanism suits a project. Use this rubric to explain substantive alignment, mismatches, and evidence gaps. The Finder uses it to compare verified candidates; the Matcher uses it for a detailed assessment of one opportunity. Other workflows may consult it when the user also requests a fit assessment.

## Evidence Needed

Use the current official opportunity requirements and researcher-supplied project facts. Attempt to establish status, deadline, eligibility, application limits, purpose, activities, award basis, project period, required partners, review criteria, and material restrictions. If opportunity evidence is incomplete, provide only a useful provisional assessment and name the limitation.

Collect project aims, methods, population, setting, stage, approximate budget, duration, team, partners, and target submission window only as needed. Ask for career-stage or other eligibility facts only when a specific requirement makes them material. Do not invent collaborators, commitments, preliminary data, or institutional capabilities.

## Assessment Dimensions

| Dimension | Compare | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Program alignment | Purpose, priorities, eligible activities, population, geography, and intended outcomes. | Distinguishes substantive fit from keyword similarity. |
| Apparent eligibility | Explicit institution and PI conditions, career-stage restrictions, prior-award rules, registrations, and application limits. | Identifies conditions that can block an otherwise relevant concept. |
| Mechanism fit | Pilot, research, training, fellowship, equipment, or other purpose; budget basis; duration; project stage. | A project can fit the topic but exceed the mechanism's scope or ceiling. |
| Team and partnership | Required disciplines, sites, mentors, community organizations, or institutional partners. | Expressions of interest are not confirmed commitments or signed letters. |
| Review-criteria fit | Published criteria and any weights compared with available evidence and plans. | Reveals content that needs development without inventing reviewer scores. |
| Feasibility and blockers | Mandatory LOI, deadlines, institutional selection, cost share, data access, required partners, and submission arrangements. | Shows whether important conditions remain unresolved before substantial drafting. |

## Classification Rules

| Classification | Use when |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Strong apparent fit** | Substantive alignment, compatible mechanism and scale, no identified blocking mismatch, and material eligibility facts established. |
| **Possible fit** | Meaningful alignment exists, but material eligibility, scope, budget, partner, institutional-selection, or feasibility questions remain unresolved or require adjustment. |
| **Weak apparent fit** | A verified rule or central program purpose conflicts with the project, or alignment depends mainly on keywords. Explain the mismatch and suggest a better search direction. |

A verified eligibility mismatch normally produces Weak apparent fit unless an authoritative exception applies. Unresolved material eligibility normally prevents Strong apparent fit. Budget mismatch may permit Possible fit when a credible scope reduction is feasible. Missing proposal-writing detail alone is not evidence of substantive mismatch.

A missed mandatory deadline is a submission blocker regardless of scientific alignment. Limited submission affects permission and feasibility; assess its effect separately from topic alignment and never imply independent submission is permitted.

## Required Explanation

Put blockers first, then provide the classification, alignment evidence, mismatches, eligibility concerns, review-criteria crosswalk, material missing facts, and three next actions. Tie comparisons to the relevant source section and supplied project fact.

Include this caution: **This classification describes alignment with the available opportunity and project information. It is not a funding prediction or final eligibility determination.**

Do not provide match percentages, award probabilities, fundability scores, predicted reviewer scores, or guarantees of eligibility or competitiveness.
