# FSU Research Routing, Limited Submissions, & Institutional Boundaries (`01_fsu_routing.md`)

Use this reference to route Florida State University grant questions to the appropriate office, distinguish institutional limits from individual PI limits, and apply FSU internal-deadline rules without making unauthorized institutional decisions.

---

## 1. Source Authority Order

1. **Current Official Sponsor Solicitation & Controlling Amendments**: Governs sponsor deadlines, eligibility, application limits, award ceilings, required components, and review criteria.
2. **Incorporated Sponsor Application Guides & FAQs**: Governs general agency formatting and submission rules unless the program solicitation specifies an exception.
3. **Current Official FSU Policy & Office of Research Guidance**: Governs FSU routing, limited-submission selection, RAMP workflows, and internal review deadlines.
4. **Researcher-Supplied Project Facts**: Describes the proposed aims, team, preliminary work, and estimated budget.
5. **Recommended Practices**: Helpful planning buffers and writing advice (always label as **Recommended Practice**, never as official FSU or sponsor policy).

## 3. FSU Limited-Submission Rules

1. **Separate Institutional Limits from Investigator Limits**:
   - **Institutional Limit**: Restricts how many applications, LOIs, concept papers, or nominations FSU (as a university, campus, school, or college) may submit.
   - **Investigator Limit**: Restricts how many applications one individual may participate in (as PI, Co-PI, or senior personnel). Report separately from institutional limits.
2. **Assign One of Six Limited-Submission Statuses**:
   - `Yes — FSU internal process identified`: Sponsor limits institutional submissions and a current-cycle FSU Limited Submissions listing is verified.
   - `Sponsor limit identified — no current FSU listing found`: Sponsor limits institutional submissions, but no matching current FSU listing is found. **Mandatory instruction**: Direct the researcher to contact **FSU Research Development** immediately; absence from the public listing does not permit independent submission.
   - `Possibly limited — institutional interpretation required`: Sponsor language is ambiguous (e.g., "one per organizational component" or shared across tracks). Refer to FSU Research Development.
   - `Investigator limit only`: Only individual participation is capped.
   - `No explicit institutional limit identified`: Current official solicitation reviewed with no institutional cap found.
   - `Unable to verify`: Official solicitation or FSU listing could not be accessed.

## 4. FSU Internal Deadline Calculation Prerequisites

Calculate a tentative FSU internal proposal deadline **only** when all four inputs below are verified:
1. **Verified Sponsor Deadline**: Exact date, time, and original time zone.
2. **FSU Applicant Role**: Whether FSU is the **lead applicant** or a **subrecipient** (when FSU is a subrecipient, FSU's deadline is governed by the pass-through entity's document deadline, not the prime sponsor deadline).
3. **Current Controlling FSU Proposal-Submission Policy**: Working-day advance-submission rule (excluding weekends).
4. **Current FSU University Closure Calendar**: Official holidays and administrative closures.

- When all four inputs are verified, label the date: **`Tentative FSU internal deadline — confirm with your departmental grants analyst and in RAMP.`**
- When any input is missing, never subtract raw calendar days or guess a date; state: **`A precise FSU internal deadline could not be calculated from the available authoritative sources; confirm timing with departmental grants staff and the responsible FSU office.`**
