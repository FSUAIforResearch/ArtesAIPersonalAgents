# Grant Compass Official Source Directory & Evidence Rules (`04_source_registry.md`)

Use this reference to prioritize authoritative FSU and external sponsor websites during live web search and solicitation verification, and to handle inaccessible or conflicting sources safely.

---

## 1. Evidence & Verification Rules

1. **Official Sponsor Solicitation Controls**: Always treat the current official solicitation (NOFO, RFP, RFA, Program Announcement) and its controlling amendments as the highest authority.
2. **Discovery Databases Are Leads Only**: Treat search engine snippets, Grants.gov summary cards, newsletters, and university reposts as discovery leads. Open or request the official solicitation before treating deadlines, eligibility, or page limits as verified.
3. **Record Verification Dates**: State the date an official webpage or document was checked. An uploaded PDF's publication date is not proof that the competition remains open unless verified live.
4. **Handle Blocked or Inaccessible Pages Transparently**:
   - If an official webpage is blocked, behind authentication, or fails to load, do **not** guess requirements or copy rules from a prior year's cycle.
   - Search for an accessible official solicitation PDF or official Grants.gov announcement record.
   - If decisive requirements remain unavailable, mark affected fields **`Unknown or Unresolved`** (`Not identified in the available authoritative sources`) and ask the user to upload the official solicitation PDF.

## 2. Official Florida State University Research Sources

| FSU Resource | Official URL | When to Consult | Scope & Limitations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FSU Office of Research** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/ | General FSU research services, announcements, and links to specialized offices. | Prefer specialized office pages below when available. |
| **SRA — Develop & Submit Proposal** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/develop-submit-proposal/ | Public-sponsor proposal components, budget guidance, subrecipient vs. contractor rules, and submission procedures. | Confirm current rates on the SRA Facts Sheet. |
| **SRA — Facts Sheet** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/sra/facts-sheet/ | Current FSU institutional identifiers, F&A (indirect cost) rates, fringe benefit rates, and tuition assumptions. | Always check the effective date of the rate table. |
| **FSU Research Foundation (FSURF)** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/fsu-research-foundation/ | Routing and policies for private foundations, nonprofits, industry, and foreign sponsors. | Confirm routing based on the flow of funds. |
| **FSU Limited Submissions** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/limitedsubmission/ | Checking internal FSU competitions and deadlines whenever a sponsor caps institutional applications. | Public list is not exhaustive; absence does not authorize independent submission. |
| **FSU Research Development (RD)** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/rd/ | Funding discovery support, limited-submission coordination, and large-proposal development services. | Contact RD directly for unlisted institutional limits. |
| **FSU Internal Funding (CRC)** | https://internalfunding.research.fsu.edu/ | Internal FSU seed grants, pilot awards, and Council on Research & Creativity programs. | Verify current cycle dates and faculty eligibility rules. |
| **Research Administration Manual** | https://adminmanual.research.fsu.edu/ | Detailed FSU research administration policies, roles, and responsibilities. | Check whether newer specific policies supersede general sections. |
| **RAMP Portal Guidance** | https://ramp.research.fsu.edu/ | Public how-to guides, training, and proposal routing workflows in RAMP. | Live proposal status requires authenticated human login. |
| **FSU RISE (Integrity, Security, Ethics)** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/rise/ | Export controls, research security, foreign influence, conflict of interest (COI), and RCR referrals. | Use to flag possible review needs, not to decide compliance. |
| **Human Subjects Protection (OHSP / IRB)** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/ohsp/ | Human participants, surveys, interviews, sensors, or identifiable private data. | Use to flag IRB referral; never declare exemption or approval. |
| **Animal Care and Use Committee (ACUC)** | https://www.research.fsu.edu/research-offices/acuc/ | Research or teaching involving vertebrate animals. | Use to flag ACUC referral; never determine oversight scope. |

## 3. Core External Discovery & Sponsor Sources

| Sponsor / Portal | Official URL | When to Consult | Verification Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grants.gov** | https://www.grants.gov/ | Broad U.S. federal grant discovery and official opportunity numbers. | Verify full details in the agency's linked NOFO/solicitation PDF. |
| **NIH Grants & Funding** | https://grants.nih.gov/ | Biomedical, behavioral, and health research NOFOs, parent announcements, and NIH Guide notices. | Check both the specific NOFO and the NIH Application Guide (SF424). |
| **NSF Funding** | https://www.nsf.gov/funding | Science, engineering, STEM education, and social/behavioral science solicitations. | Check both the program solicitation and the **NSF PAPPG** (`https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg`). |
| **Institute of Education Sciences (IES)** | https://ies.ed.gov/funding | Education and special-education research and training competitions. | Verify the current fiscal-year request for applications (RFA) and application guide. |
| **National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH)** | https://www.neh.gov/grants | Humanities research, fellowships, preservation, and digital/public humanities grants. | Check program-specific NOFOs and sample application narratives. |
| **National Endowment for the Arts (NEA)** | https://www.arts.gov/grants | Arts projects, research labs, and community arts partnerships. | Verify applicant eligibility and cost-share/matching rules carefully. |
| **USDA NIFA** | https://www.nifa.usda.gov/grants | Agriculture, food systems, nutrition, rural community, and natural resource grants. | The current RFA controls cost-sharing and priority area rules. |
| **DOE Office of Science** | https://science.osti.gov/grants | Physical sciences, energy, computing, and environmental system FOAs. | Check whether preapplications or LOIs are mandatory in PAMS. |
| **NASA NSPIRES** | https://nspires.nasaprs.com/ | Space, Earth science, aerospace, and STEM solicitations (including ROSES). | If dynamic NSPIRES pages block retrieval, ask the user to upload the solicitation PDF. |
| **HRSA Grants** | https://www.hrsa.gov/grants | Rural health, health workforce, maternal/child health, and clinical training grants. | Verify facility/geographic eligibility and the current NOFO. |
